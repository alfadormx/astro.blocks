The demos below render their headline as `h2` and tier names as `h3`, because the page already has an `h1`. A `yearly` price is shown as an annual total; Pricing does not divide it. Numeric prices are formatted in the page language with `Intl.NumberFormat`; string prices render verbatim.

## Three tiers

`highlighted` emphasises one tier with a primary border, a shadow and a solid action, and `badge` adds a pill on its top edge. When tiers stack on narrow widths, the highlighted tier moves first.

## Billing toggle

When any tier has a `yearly` price, a Monthly/Yearly toggle renders above the tiers. It works without JavaScript; with it, the change is announced to screen readers.

<Example name="billing-toggle" source={billingToggleSource}>
  <BillingToggle />
</Example>

## Mixed tiers

Tiers without a `yearly` price keep their price in both billing states.
