Hero renders its title as an `h1` by default, because it opens the page. The examples below pass `headline.htmlTag: 'h2'` only because this page already has its own `h1`. Leave it out when Hero is the first block.

## Background video

`container.background` puts an image or video behind centered copy, with a `bg-base/60` overlay by default. Setting `container.background.overlayClass` replaces that overlay rather than merging with it, so `''` removes it. `container.minHeight` sets the height. A video gets a pause/play button and stays paused under reduced motion.

<Example name="background-video" source={backgroundVideoSource}>
  <BackgroundVideo />
</Example>

## Bottom-aligned copy

`contentPosition: 'end'` puts the copy at the bottom of a hero taller than its content, such as one with `container.minHeight`. Without a min-height the hero is only as tall as its copy, so the setting has nothing to do. It applies to `layout: 'centered'` only.

<Example name="bottom-aligned" source={bottomAlignedSource}>
  <BottomAligned />
</Example>

## Split, media on the right

`layout: 'split'` puts the copy beside media: set `image` or `video`. On small screens the copy stacks above the media.

## Split, media on the left

`mediaPosition: 'left'` moves the media to the left column from the `md` breakpoint up. `twoColumnContainer` sets the ratio and gap.
