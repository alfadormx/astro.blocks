## Listening to `selection:change`

With a `group`, the field declares a `text` selection group and publishes its raw value on every keystroke as a single entry, `[{ code: text }]`. An empty field publishes `[]`. Values published by others are shown in the field; one longer than `maxLength` is truncated and republished.
