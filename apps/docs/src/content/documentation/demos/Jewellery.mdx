Atelier Halden is a fictional jeweller in Antwerp, and its site is one of the demo sites built only from this library. It is the most interactive of the demos: a video hero, collections behind tabs, and a ring configurator where you pick a model, stone, metal and engraving on a live 3D ring and share the result as a link. This guide walks the home page from top to bottom, then the collections, configurator, about and contact pages, and pulls each section apart into the blocks it is made of. ## The big picture

The site has five pages: home, collections, the ring configurator, about and contact, in English and Spanish. Every page shares one layout with a light sticky header and a quiet footer. Its look is an atelier: warm paper, warm near-black ink, a single deep burgundy, hairline borders, square corners, a high-contrast serif for headings and small tracked capitals for labels.

### Things that are shared by every page

- **Theme by tokens.** `_theme.css` overrides semantic tokens on `[data-demo='jewellery']`: a warm `background`, white `surface`, near-black `normal`, a burgundy `primary` and a deeper burgundy `tertiary` for the dark band, with every `--radius-*` set to `0`. Cormorant Garamond sets the headings and Instrument Sans the body. Its dark theme is a separate set of warm darks, written out in the same file. Read [Colors](/documentation/base/Colors) for the token names.
- **Shared prop objects.** `_site.ts` exports `SECTION_HEADLINE` (a centred heading with a tracked eyebrow and serif title), `LABEL` (the small tracked capitals class string), the navigation, and `RING_PRESETS`, the configurator codes for the rings on the collections page.
- **Links into the configurator.** `configuratorUrl()` builds `/configurator?code=…`, so any button on the site can open the configurator with a ring already chosen.
- **Copy by language.** Every word lives in `src/copy/jewellery/en.yaml` and `es.yaml`, read with `useCopy(Astro, 'jewellery')`, including the configurator's option names.
- **Invented content.** The jeweller, its history, its customers and its team are placeholders; nothing is for sale.

## The home page, from top to bottom

### 1. Header and menu

**What you see:** a light bar that stays at the top, with the serif brand name in the middle, two links on each side, and a theme toggle. On phones the links fold into a menu that slides in from the right.

**Built from:** [Header](/documentation/composite/Header) with `isSticky`, a `bg-background/95 backdrop-blur` background and a bottom hairline. A burger opens a [Modal](/documentation/layout/Modal) with a [NavigationTree](/documentation/composite/NavigationTree) of all four links.

## The other pages

### 8. Collections

<figure>
  <Img08CollectionsPage
    role="img"
    aria-label={'The collections page, pulled apart into its layers'}
    style={{ width: '100%', height: 'auto' }}
  />
</figure>

**What you see:** Rings, Necklaces and Earrings as three tabs across the page; each tab shows its pieces as portrait photos, and every ring has a "Design this ring" link.

**Built from:** a Headline and [Tabs](/documentation/composite/Tabs) whose `tabList` is a three-column grid with no gap, so the tabs fill the width. `defaultItemConfig` makes each tab a square ghost button with a hairline underneath; `activeItemConfig` thickens the line under the selected one. Each panel is an [ItemsGrid](/documentation/composite/ItemsGrid) passed into a `panel-<n>` slot. The ring links come from `RING_PRESETS`, so each one opens the configurator on that ring.

### 9. Ring configurator

<figure>
  <Img09Configurator
    role="img"
    aria-label={'The configurator, pulled apart into its layers'}
    style={{ width: '100%', height: 'auto' }}
  />
</figure>

**What you see:** a 3D ring you can turn, with a title and a short summary of your choices above it; on the right, six categories (model, carat, metal, clarity, extras, engraving) as icon tabs, each opening a grid of options. Every choice changes the ring at once, and the address bar keeps a code you can share.

**Built from:** one [Configurator](/documentation/composite/Configurator). It lays a [ModelViewer](/documentation/primitives/ModelViewer) (the GLB ring, a studio HDR environment, bloom and a camera preset that turns to the band for engraving) beside Tabs of `categories`. Each category lists `options`, and each option lists `mutations`: swap the model, show a different stone, recolour the band, or change the stone's material. Extras use `mode: 'multiple'` with a `max`; engraving uses a text panel. `summary` writes the chosen metal, carat and clarity under the title, `defaultItemsGridConfig` and `selectedItemConfig` draw the hairline option tiles, and `urlState` keeps the selection in the URL. [Composing blocks](/documentation/guides/Composing) explains the layers this page leans on most.
