A grid of person cards: photo, name, role, bio and social links. Team is a preset over ItemsGrid, so every member is an Item: the name is `title`, the role and bio are `content` (HTML), the photo is `image` and the links are `actions`.

## Basic

Names render as `h3` headings and photos are decorative (`alt=""`), because the name sits right next to them. Icon-only links have no visible text, so give each one an `aria-label` naming the person, such as "Ana on LinkedIn".
