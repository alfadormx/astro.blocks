## Previous and next

`prevButton` and `nextButton` are Buttons; each renders when set. Its `subtitle` is the page title, shown under a small "Previous" or "Next".

<Example name="both" source={bothSource}>
  <Both />
</Example>

## Next only

A lone next link sits at the inline end: right in left-to-right text, left in right-to-left.

<Example name="lone-next" source={loneNextSource}>
  <LoneNext />
</Example>

## Previous only

A lone previous link sits at the inline start.

<Example name="lone-prev" source={lonePrevSource}>
  <LonePrev />
</Example>

## Long titles

The row wraps, and a long title wraps inside its link.

<Example name="long-titles" source={longTitlesSource}>
  <LongTitles />
</Example>

## Custom labels

`ariaLabel` names the landmark; each link's `label` replaces the built-in text. `defaultButtonConfig` is merged under both links.

<Example name="custom-labels" source={customLabelsSource}>
  <CustomLabels />
</Example>

## Slot

The default slot renders between the links. Here the row is centred, and `class: 'ms-0'` on `nextButton` cancels its inline-end default.
